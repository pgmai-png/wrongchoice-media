Privacy Policy · Wrong Choice Uploader

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Wrong Choice Uploader is a private tool used only by the creator of the Wrong Choice horror channel to send the channel's own videos to its own TikTok account as drafts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it uses

  • The access token that TikTok issues when the channel's account authorizes the tool, stored only on the creator's own computer.
  • The account's basic profile information (open ID, display name) that TikTok returns, used only to confirm which account receives the drafts.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any data about other TikTok users.
  • It does not read messages, followers or analytics.
  • It does not use cookies or tracking.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services). Revoking it deletes the tool's access immediately.

Contact

Through the channel's Instagram account, @wrongchoicehorror.
